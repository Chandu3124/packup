COOKIES POLICY

How We Use Cookies

Packup uses cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences over time.

2. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to work properly. They support core features such as page navigation, secure areas, and order processing.

3. Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices such as dark mode, language direction, and other display settings so you do not need to select them again.

4. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are most visited. This information is used to improve website performance.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website features and preferences.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at hello@packup.com .
