LUGGAGE CARE GUIDE

Simple Care for Longer Use

Regular care can help maintain your bag's appearance and functionality. Clean materials according to their care instructions, avoid overpacking, keep wheels and zippers free of debris, and store luggage in a clean, dry place.

01

Clean Carefully

Follow the material's care instructions and use suitable cleaning methods to remove dirt gently.

Gentle cleaning
02

Pack Sensibly

Distribute items evenly and avoid overpacking to help protect your bag, handles, and closures.

Balanced packing
03

Check Moving Parts

Keep wheels, zippers, and handles free of debris and check them periodically for visible issues.

Routine inspection
04

Store Properly

Store your luggage in a clean, dry place and make sure it is dry before putting it away.

Dry storage

Care tip: Always follow the care instructions provided with your specific luggage or travel bag.
